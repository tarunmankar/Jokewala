This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
**Jokewala does NOT collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.**
- We do NOT require user account creation or login.
- We do NOT access your contacts, camera, photos, microphone, or precise location.
- All jokes, categories, and user favorites are stored locally on your device via SQLite.

## 2. Text-to-Speech Feature
The application utilizes your device's built-in text-to-speech engine (via Expo Speech) to read jokes aloud. No voice data or recordings are captured, processed on remote servers, or stored by us.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Jokewala is completely offline-friendly. It does not integrate third-party ad networks or user tracking SDKs.
If you share a joke via third-party platforms (such as WhatsApp, Instagram, or messaging apps), the data shared is governed by the respective third-party service's privacy policy.

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 5. Security
Because the app does not transmit any personal data over the internet, your personal privacy is completely preserved on your device.
