## Subcategory: `Wedding Butter Naan`

### Joke `HJ-000105`
- **Format**: `monologue` | **Language**: `hinglish` | **Clean**: `Yes`
- **Source**: [Times of India & Duniyahaigol](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/humour/jokes/wedding-naan-kundali-and-gym-funny-chutkule)

Shaadi ke buffet me ladke ne daba-daba kar 10 butter naan pel diye. Aadhi raat ko pet me aisi aag lagi ki bathroom me baith kar bol pada: 'Hey bhagwan, ya toh jaan nikaal le, ya naan nikaal le!'

## Subcategory: `Kundali 36 Gun`

### Joke `HJ-000106`
- **Format**: `dialogue` | **Language**: `hinglish` | **Clean**: `Yes`
- **Source**: [Times of India & Duniyahaigol](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/humour/jokes/wedding-naan-kundali-and-gym-funny-chutkule)

```text
Pandit ji ne kundali milayi toh 36 ke 36 gun match ho gaye! Phir bhi ladke ke baap ne shaadi se saaf inkaar kar diya.
Ladki wale (hairaan hokar): Jab 36 gun mil rahe hain toh mana kyu kar rahe hain?
Ladke ka baap: Hamara beta toh poora nalayak aur awara hai, ab kya bahu bhi bilkul aisi hi le aayein!
```
