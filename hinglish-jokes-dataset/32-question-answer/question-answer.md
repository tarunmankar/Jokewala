## Subcategory: `Lemon Tree River`

### Joke `HJ-000135`
- **Format**: `dialogue` | **Language**: `hinglish` | **Clean**: `Yes`
- **Source**: [ScoopWhoop & Quora & Amar Ujala](https://www.scoopwhoop.com/humour/17-good-bad-hindi-puns-and-desi-wordplay)

```text
Question: Nadi ke beech me nimbu ka ped laga hai, nimbu kaise todoge?
Answer: Chidiya ban kar ud kar todunga!
Question: Par tumhe chidiya kaun banayega?
Answer: Wahi jo nadi ke beech me nimbu ka ped lagayega!
```
