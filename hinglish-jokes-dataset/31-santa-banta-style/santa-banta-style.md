## Subcategory: `Empty Stomach Banana`

### Joke `HJ-000132`
- **Format**: `dialogue` | **Language**: `hinglish` | **Clean**: `Yes`
- **Source**: [Amar Ujala & LiveHindustan](https://www.amarujala.com/humour-jokes/train-traffic-exams-pappu-and-santa-banta-chutkule)

```text
Banta: Santa tu khaali pet kitne kele kha sakta hai?
Santa: Main 6 kele kha sakta hoon.
Banta: Galat! Pehla kela khane ke baad pet khaali kahan bacha! Isliye tu bas ek hi kela kha sakta hai.
Santa ghar gaya aur biwi se poochha: Tu khaali pet kitne kele kha sakti hai?
Biwi: Main 4 kha sakti hoon.
Santa: Dhhatt teri ki! Agar 6 bolti toh ek mast joke sunata!
```
