## Subcategory: `Train Ticket Proof`

### Joke `HJ-000120`
- **Format**: `dialogue` | **Language**: `hinglish` | **Clean**: `Yes`
- **Source**: [Amar Ujala & LiveHindustan](https://www.amarujala.com/humour-jokes/train-traffic-exams-pappu-and-santa-banta-chutkule)

```text
TT ne platform par pakad liya: Ticket dikhao!
Yatri: Arre main train me baith kar aaya hi nahi!
TT: Is baat ka kya saboot hai tumhare paas?
```
