## Subcategory: `Bahubali Leave`

### Joke `HJ-000064`
- **Format**: `dialogue` | **Language**: `hinglish` | **Clean**: `Yes`
- **Source**: [LiveHindustan & Amar Ujala](https://www.amarujala.com/humour-jokes/boss-and-employee-funny-jokes-in-hindi-leave-chutkule)

```text
Employee: Sir, mujhe 4 din ki chhutti chahiye.
Boss: Chhutti ek shart par milegi, ye batao ki Katappa ne Bahubali ko kyu maara tha?

Boss: Kitne din ki chhutti chahiye bolo?
```
