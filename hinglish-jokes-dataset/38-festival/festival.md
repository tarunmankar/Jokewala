## Subcategory: `Soan Papdi Circle`

### Joke `HJ-000153`
- **Format**: `dialogue` | **Language**: `hinglish` | **Clean**: `Yes`
- **Source**: [Times of India & Jagran](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/humour/jokes/diwali-soan-papdi-circulation-and-gully-cricket-rules)

```text
Padosi: Bhaisahab, ye lijiye Diwali ki shubhkaamnaayein aur ye meetha tohfa!
Hum (dabba dekh kar man me): Arre nahi! Ye toh wahi Soan Papdi ka dabba hai jo pichhle saal maine Sharma ji ko diya tha, Sharma ji ne Verma ji ko aur Verma ji ne inko diya tha!
```
