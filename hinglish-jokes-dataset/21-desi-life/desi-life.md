## Subcategory: `Chai Hospitality`

### Joke `HJ-000102`
- **Format**: `dialogue` | **Language**: `hinglish` | **Clean**: `Yes`
- **Source**: [ScoopWhoop & MensXP](https://www.scoopwhoop.com/humour/middle-class-habits-and-desi-shopping-jokes)

```text
Desi mehmaannawazi ka niyam: Agar koi ghar par aaye aur puchhe 'Chai piyoge?' aur aap bol dein 'Nahi shukriya', toh wo phir bhi ek kadak cup bana kar saamne rakh denge aur bolenge: 'Thodi si pee lo, ab toh ban hi gayi hai'!
```
